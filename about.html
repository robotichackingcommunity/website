About

Robotic Hacking Community is a DEF CON community focused on the security of physical AI systems and robotics. Machines that move, sense, decide, and act in the real world.

We bring together security researchers, hackers, and engineers to explore the attack surface of intelligent machines. The field is new. The research is sparse. The deployment is fast.

Come find what defenders miss.

// Venue Las Vegas Convention Center  ·  West Hall 4  ·  Level 1  ·  Room 1309
// who this is for

Never Hacked a Robot Before?
Good Starting Point.

If you've done web, binary, hardware, or embedded security, the skills transfer. The target is new. That's the point.

We run workshops that start from first principles. The CTF is open to all experience levels. The talks are for anyone who wants to understand how these systems fail.

Deep in robot security research or just picking it up. Either way, this is where the work is.

// community-driven

No Vendors. No Pitches.
Just Research.

Robotic Hacking Community is run by researchers, for researchers. We are not a product showcase. Nothing here is for sale.

Every talk is reviewed on technical merit. Every workshop is hands-on. The CTF runs on real systems. If something is here, it earned its place.
