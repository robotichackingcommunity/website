Rogue Robot
Challenge

Robots are shipping faster than the security around them. Every two weeks, we hand the community a new way to prove it — make any AI-powered robot break its own rules, on camera, in front of everyone.

The Rogue Robot Challenge takes attack categories that usually live in research papers and turns them into something anyone can watch happen in real time. If a hacker can pull this off for a prize, an attacker can pull it off for profit. The gap between "funny clip" and "security incident" is smaller than most people think.

Five challenges. Five real Physical AI attack vectors, made visible.

// mechanics
  • Any robot, any device. Any robot. Any AI-powered device.
  • Any method. Prompt injection, sensor manipulation, social engineering — all fair game.
  • Record everything. Sound or movement must be clearly observable on camera.
  • Submit your entry by posting the clip on X with:
    #RogueRobotChallenge #RogueRobotChallengeWeek1 #RHC2026
  • RHC reposts the strongest entries.
  • Most shares on the RHC repost wins.
