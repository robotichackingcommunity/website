Robotic Hacking Community Launches at DEF CON 34

First dedicated robotics village brings hands-on robot exploitation and physical AI security to the hacker community.

The Robotic Hacking Community debuted as the official robotics village at DEF CON 34 in Las Vegas, offering hands-on workshops, live demonstrations, hardware challenges, and talks on physical AI and robotics cybersecurity. Participants explored real robot systems, discovered vulnerabilities in autonomous platforms, and collaborated on exploit development in a live lab environment.

The village featured talks by leading researchers, CTF competitions with robot hardware prizes, and fireside chats with industry leaders from Boston Dynamics and VicOne discussing the future of Physical AI security.
