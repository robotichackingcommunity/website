Fireside Chat

Physical AI turns cybersecurity into a product safety and business readiness requirement. At the Robotic Hacking Community at DEF CON 34, Joman Chu, Product Security Engineering Lead at Boston Dynamics, and Jason Yueh, CTO of VicOne, discuss what changes when intelligent machines perceive, decide, and act in the physical world.

The fireside chat examines how cybersecurity fits across the robot lifecycle — from design and simulation to deployment and continuous monitoring.

  • How do you know whether a robot that is still operating is also still safe?
  • How can simulation reveal whether a cyber threat could change real-world robot behavior before deployment?
  • What happens when a new vulnerability changes the assumptions behind an existing safety case?
  • How should robotics companies maintain cyber-safety assurance as software, AI models, dependencies, and threats continue to evolve?
